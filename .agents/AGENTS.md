# MakeMyStay Content Generation Rules (Topical Authority Clusters)

When asked to generate blog content or guides for MakeMyStay.ai, do not generate standalone isolated posts. Always build and expand upon **Topical Authority Clusters** designed to be the best on the internet.

For every article generated, you MUST follow these constraints:

1. **Research & Uniqueness**: Research the current top 10 ranking pages before writing. Identify missing topics and include them. Never paraphrase competitors—add unique value.
2. **Length & Depth**: Produce 3,500–5,000 words of original, helpful content.
3. **Mandatory Inclusions**:
   - Original comparison tables
   - Rental trend tables
   - Cost-of-living breakdowns
   - Commute times to major IT parks and metro connectivity
   - Nearby companies, schools, hospitals, malls, and restaurants
   - Pros and cons
   - Best PG and budget recommendations
   - Local insights that are difficult to find elsewhere
   - 10–15 FAQs targeting Google AI Overviews
4. **Technical SEO**: Add Article, FAQ, Breadcrumb, and other relevant JSON-LD schema.
5. **Linking & Visuals**: Suggest 10–15 contextual internal links to existing and future pages. Suggest original images, infographics, and maps.
6. **CTA**: End with a strong CTA that naturally links to relevant MakeMyStay.ai listings.
7. **Output Explanation**: For every article, output an explanation covering:
   - Why it can rank.
   - Which keywords it targets.
   - Which existing pages it should internally link to.
   - Which future pages should be created to strengthen the content cluster.
